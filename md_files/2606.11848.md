abstract: The second solar spectrum is the spectrum of the Stokes parameter Q (linear
  polarization) close to the solar limb. It is made of a few polarized lines with
  Q/I of about 1% (such as CaI, SrI, or BaII), but most lines exhibit weaker polarization.
  This paper presents processing of unpublished observations made in 2008 with the
  Meudon solar tower spectropolarimeter, which are of interest for weak and turbulent
  unresolved magnetic field measurements in the quiet Sun, through the Hanle effect.
