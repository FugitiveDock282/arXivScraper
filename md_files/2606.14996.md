abstract: Motivated by Grand Unification, we study the formation of magnetic monopoles
  in an SU(3) non-Abelian gauge theory. We find that the number density of magnetic
  monopoles depends critically on a parameter, $ε$ , that controls the abundance and
  subsequent decay of biased domain walls. For sufficiently small but non-vanishing
  values of $ε$ , very few monopoles and walls survive in our simulations, potentially
  solving the cosmological monopole over-abundance problem. In addition, the scenario
  predicts a stochastic gravitational background from biased domain walls and the
  possibility of magnetically charged black holes.
