abstract: Using Information Theory, we investigate the co-evolution of bars and spiral
  arms in barred-spiral galaxies from the cosmological magneto-hydrodynamic Illustris
  TNG50 simulations. We first calculate Mutual Information (MI) between a structural
  or kinematic parameter of the bar (bar strength $A_{2bar}$ , bar length $r_{bar}$
  , bar pattern speed $Ω$ ) and that of a spiral arm (spiral strength $A_{2spiral}$
  , spiral arm pitch angle $Ψ$ ). We calculate MI in two different galaxy samples...
  (i) one forming bars before spirals (ii) other forming spirals before bars. We note,
  spirals form immediately after bars in the first sample, whereas bars form 1.7 Gyrs
  after spirals in the second. We find a high mean MI value in each of these samples
  (0.4 - 0.5), and in the combined sample (0.4-0.8), confirming a fair degree of association
  of the bar and the spiral arm. To identify whether the bar or the spiral arm effectively
  drives their co-evolution, we calculate the Transfer Entropy (TE) (bar-to-spiral
  TE, spiral-to-bar TE), from the time series data of each of the above bar-spiral
  parameter pairs. We find that the median bar-to-spiral TE and spiral-to-bar TE values
  vary between $ 0.33$ and $ 0.42$ for each galaxy sample, comparable to those of
  the combined sample. A similar trend was observed in our calculated Liang information
  flow rates. Our novel approach may possibly indicate that the bar and the spiral
  arm regulate their co-evolution on an equal footing.
