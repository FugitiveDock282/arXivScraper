abstract: The magnetohydrodynamic (MHD) equilibrium $β$ limits in the Chinese First
  QuasiAxisymmetric Stellarator (CFQS) are investigated using the NTEC code, for both
  the standard and the magnetic island configurations. The equilibrium $β$ limit is
  identified upon the onset of the rapid destruction of nested flux surfaces by evaluating
  several numerical metrics, including the fractal dimension, weighted Birkhoff average,
  and effective volume of parallel diffusion. In the standard configuration, the net-current-free
  and the bootstrap-current-carrying equilibria can sustain well-ordered magnetic
  surfaces up to $\langleβ\rangle\approx1.5\%$ . The proliferation of stochastic field
  lines starts after the critical overlap between the internal major islands and the
  high-order island chains. Two types of divertor island configurations are studied
  based on net-current-free equilibria. It is found that the edge islands may transition
  into open field lines at low $\langleβ\rangle$ values and lead to a drastic shrinkage
  of the last closed flux surface. Meanwhile, the threshold $\langleβ\rangle$ value
  of the degradation of inner flux surfaces is similar to the standard configuration.
