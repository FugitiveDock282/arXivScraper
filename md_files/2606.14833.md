abstract: We searched for past close encounters of the Hyades and Pleiades with all
  5736 clusters of the Hunt & Reffert catalog, integrating orbits with galpy over
  the last 100 Myr and validating candidates by Monte Carlo. Most nominally close
  pairs are unstable under catalog errors; only two remain reliable... Pleiades and
  HSC 751 and Hyades and HSC 2986. HSC 2986 coincides with the star-forming cloud
  Corona Australis, which the Hyades crossed about 5 Myr ago, passing ~11 pc from
  its dense core. Such interactions may be the cause of the mini-clusters discovered
  in the Pleiades.
