abstract: Electrostatic parametric instabilities in the inhomogeneous near-SOL tokamak
  plasma, driven by the combined action of a high-harmonic fast wave (HHFW) with a
  frequency near the 30th ion-cyclotron (IC) harmonic and electron and ion temperature
  gradients, are investigated numerically. The results indicate the parametric decay
  of the HHFW into a HHIC (Bernstein) wave and HHIC quasimode. The instability is
  found to exist within a finite wavelength range of the HHFW. The development of
  the parametric HHIC quasimode decay instability leads to the onset of parametric
  turbulence accompanied by anisotropic ion heating, with the ion heating rate across
  the magnetic field significantly exceeding that along the magnetic field.
