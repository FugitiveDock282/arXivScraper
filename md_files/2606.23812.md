abstract: The joint observation of astrophysical sources in gamma rays and neutrinos
  can provide invaluable insight into the physical conditions of the source, including
  its size, particle densities, and acceleration and production mechanisms. In this
  work, we investigate the role of nuclear composition in high-energy astrophysical
  environments. Using NGC 1068 as a representative example, we perform detailed Monte
  Carlo simulations of nuclear and electromagnetic cascades within the source and
  study the imprints of the injected nuclear composition on the resulting neutrino
  and gamma-ray emissions. We further discuss the importance of MeV-GeV gamma-ray
  observations for constraining the source composition in the context of future gamma-ray
  experiments. A dedicated re-analysis of archival COMPTEL observations is also presented.
