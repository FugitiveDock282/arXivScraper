abstract: The second solar spectrum is the spectrum of the Stokes parameter Q (linear
  polarization) close to the solar limb. It differs significantly of the usual intensity
  spectrum (Stokes parameter I). The second solar spectrum contains in the blue just
  a few polarized lines with Q/I of about 1% (such as CaI, SrI, SrII, BaII, most lines
  exhibit much weaker polarization. This paper presents new processing of observations
  made in 2004-2006 with the Pic du Midi Turret Dome spectropolarimeter, which are
  of strong interest for weak and turbulent unresolved magnetic field measurements
  in the quiet Sun, through the interpretation of the Hanle effect. As the slit was
  orthogonal to the limb, the polarization rate Q/I is determined precisely and continuously
  up to 80 distance of the limb.
