abstract: In this paper we consider quark star solutions to Liu et al.s \cite{Liu_2019}
  quasi-topological electromagnetism (QTEM), a recently proposed form of dark energy.
  Since the QTEM contribution is trivial for pure electric/magnetic charge, we consider
  the dyonic case in pure QTEM which does induce (dark) non-trivial dynamics from
  the non-linear theory. Besides the introduction of a dyonic charge distribution
  generally pushing the characteristic quark star `hook shape to larger masses and
  radii, it also induces a second branch at very large mass and radius for stars with
  a small dyonic charge ratio. This second set of solutions have a negative pressure
  envelope surrounding a positive pressure core. As we explore the parameter space
  these features interact and evolve in interesting ways, with the two branches eventually
  merging in $M/R$ space before settling into a characteristic `paperclip shape as
  the dyonic charge ratio becomes large.
