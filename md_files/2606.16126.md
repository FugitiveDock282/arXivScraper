abstract: The frictional force arising from the interaction of ions with a magnetized
  electron beam in a strong magnetic field is investigated. The problem is reduced
  to the interaction of quasiparticles, or Larmor circles, with ions. A Hamiltonian
  describing this interaction is derived. The role of various impact parameters in
  the process of ion cooling is elucidated. The cases of both positively and negatively
  charged ions are considered.
